Random Generators

Passwords, passphrases, numbers, dice, picks and shuffles. Every draw comes from the browser's cryptographic random source, not Math.random.

i Everything here uses crypto.getRandomValues with rejection sampling, so the results are uniform and unpredictable — the difference that matters when the output is a password. Nothing is generated on, or sent to, a server.
