The ones people always look up

301 vs 302 vs 307 vs 308 — which redirect?

CodePermanent?Method & body preserved?Cacheable by default?Use it when…
301YesNot guaranteed — some clients turn a POST into a GETYesA GET-safe page moved for good
302NoNot guaranteed — same historical quirkNoA GET-safe page moved temporarily
307NoGuaranteedNoA temporary redirect that must keep the exact method and body, e.g. a POST
308YesGuaranteedYesA permanent redirect that must keep the exact method and body

iRule of thumb: reach for 307/308 unless you specifically need the older, looser 301/302 behaviour — 307 and 308 are the pair that guarantee the request is repeated exactly as sent.

401 vs 403 — who's being told no?

CodeMeansFixResponse must include

403 ForbiddenYou are authenticated, but not allowed to do thisNothing, as this identity — you need different permissionsNo specific header required

iIf re-sending the same credentials could ever fix it, that's 401. If the server would say no no matter who signs in with this account, that's 403.
