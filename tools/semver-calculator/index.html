Range syntax reference
SyntaxMeaning
^1.2.3Compatible: the leftmost non-zero part stays fixed, everything to the right can grow.
~1.2.3Approximately: patch can grow, minor and major stay fixed.
1.2.x / 1.2.*Any patch release of 1.2.
*Any version at all.
>=1.2.3 <2.0.0Space-separated comparators are ANDed together.
1.2.3 - 2.3.4Inclusive hyphen range.
1.2.7 || >=1.4.0Either side of || can satisfy the range.
